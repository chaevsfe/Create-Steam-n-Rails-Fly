Fixes for the Create Fly 26.2 port. All changes tested against a real 26.2 dedicated server.

- Advancements no longer reset every time you rejoin
- Track buffers, headstocks and crossings keep their colour and material
- Track casings no longer disappear when you break a curve
- Switches and couplers keep their state across a restart
- Signals and automatic switches work again
- A crash mid-drive no longer disables every switch until restart
- Players no longer get kicked when cycling item tags
- Toolbox packets validate the slot they are given
- Restored 274 missing recipes, including the fuel tank, portable fuel interface, benchcart, jukeboxcart, conductor vent, and the locometal flywheels and ladders

Requires Create Fly 6.0.9-1 and Java 25.
